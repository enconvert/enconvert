Verified cause (adversarial review 2026-06-06): crawl4ai 0.8.9
passes --disable-blink-features=AutomationControlled UNCONDITIONALLY
at browser launch (crawl4ai browser_manager.py build_browser_flags /
_build_browser_args), so webdriver suppression is browser-process-wide
and raw get_context()/new_page() pages inherit it. The flag is not
even gated on enable_stealth.
Caveat: only this launch-flag layer is global. The per-page
playwright_stealth JS overlay (_apply_stealth_to_page) runs ONLY on
arun() pages; raw pages lack UA/languages/permissions coherence
(observed: permissions 'prompt' vs 'default', different UA strings).
Raw-context pages remain PARTIALLY stealthed until Group F migrates
V1 converters to arun(); the get_page_with_stealth() shim remains
the documented fallback (plan section A2).
