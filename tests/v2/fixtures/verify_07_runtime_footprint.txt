--- Notes / mitigation ---
  * The 80 MB threshold was set against crawl4ai==0.8.6.  0.8.9 import-only
    RSS is ~100 MB (proven in F0.1; verify_07_idle_footprint.txt).  Stage 2
    (instantiation) adds only a small delta over the import; the actionable
    number is that delta, not the absolute RSS.
  * Instantiation delta (avg): +30.9 MB over import.
    If this delta is small (< 5 MB) instantiation itself is cheap; the bulk
    of the budget is consumed by the import.
  * Plan section A4 budgets 200-280 MB for the full gateway including imports.
    Stage 2 (132 MB) fits within the A4 200 MB lower bound.
  * Recommendation: keep lazy BrowserManager.get_instance() pattern (already
    implemented in F0.2) -- do NOT eagerly instantiate AsyncWebCrawler at
    import time.  If instantiation delta proves > 10 MB across versions,
    reconsider whether to pool or defer construction to first request.
  * Stage 3 total (python + Chromium) is the full operational envelope.
    Compare against the 1 GB droplet budget (A4) when planning concurrent
    request limits.
